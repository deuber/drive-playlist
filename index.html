    Before you leave

    1. 1Tap Open in Spotify on each stop, pick the matching episode, and tap the download arrow. Then mark it Downloaded here.
    2. 2In Spotify, check Your Library → Downloaded to confirm everything is there.
    3. 3Turn on Airplane Mode for a minute and play a few seconds of each one. If it plays, it's saved offline.
    4. 4If an episode isn't on Spotify, use the backup link (Apple Podcasts or the Lex Fridman site) and download it there.

    Spotify lets you download podcast episodes on free and Premium accounts. Long episodes like the 4-hour AI show take a few hundred MB, so download on Wi-Fi. Durations are approximate; check them in the player.
